## Semantic checks

1. In the water O6 export `established=1` implies physical service, trust, freshness and two matching readings. The Merwin correspondence covers all three electrical polls. The cost correspondence covers its final reading. All five positive `V/v2_*/O6_correspondence.kyp` records also establish the evidence flag itself.
2. An implication from a credible observation to a matching physical quantity is compatible with always withholding evidence. The positive models prove both conjuncts on the same completed executions.
3. The O2 diamonds and O3 boxes serve different purposes. `paper_v2/formal.py:audit_prefixes` checks successors for every admitted finite semantic prefix. Its reachable outcomes exclude the auxiliary values rejected by the guard that defines that same assignment. The O3 exports check the actual terminal clock. Three scans, ordered hydro polls at a fixed deadline, or mandatory tank phases totaling the horizon exclude skipping the schedule on positive inputs.
4. O4, O5 and O7 share complete-program exports where the matrix records conjunct projections. Positive conclusions also require exclusion of persistent failure, since O5 allows failure explicitly. O6 is a separate correspondence/production projection. The water and hydro integrity theorems concern their modeled measurements.
5. M13 and MER10 have reachable adverse paths in the widened model. Those witnesses refute its universal service guarantee. The unresolved incident cannot identify which dependency relation applies.
6. M5 independently chooses a delivered bit once and holds it over the three scans. Its specific diamond reaches the stipulated suppressed branch from a stopped pump.
7. H1 disables a required actor. H2 admits an authorized operator ready one tick after origin, completes all three scans at tick 4 and misses the tick-3 deadline. Its sampled flow is still 4 L/s at tick 3. O5 and O6 can hold for the terminal physical state and meter even when the scheduled boundary (O3) and timed conjunction (O7) fail. H1 and H2 have separate records.
8. The hydro reduction checks 1,296 pinned plant steps. At polls 1285, 1291 and 1296 a failed electrical contact yields three zero outputs even though the close command is one. Both adverse diamonds bind the final service flag to zero.
9. `milestone2/chp_device_evidence.json`, `milestone2/chp.py` and `results/milestone2/chp_fail_closed_results.json` bind reset, boot, reviewed backup reload and benign execution to a bounded device fixture. No process/output dynamics or customer observation is bound. CHP0 has open process and customer claims. CHP6 explicitly falsifies a process prerequisite.
10. Dualizing the disturbance gives Demon the initial loss choice. Truthful availability is revealed before Angel chooses a guarded route or hold. The strategy postcondition covers safety and service through the benchmark horizon.

The assessment counts, timing ranges, and sensitivity summaries in evaluation are generated measurements or arithmetic over the supplied assessments. They introduce no additional dL or dGL theorem.
