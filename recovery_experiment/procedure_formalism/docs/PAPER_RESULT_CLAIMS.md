# Paper result claims

## SUPPORTED NOW

- In 54 labeled scenarios with 68 candidate assessments, B4 established 11 positive-service guarantees, rejected 51 and returned six unresolved outcomes. The union of assessments retained by a weaker baseline with no end-to-end assurance contains 53 rows. [RQ1 table](../results/milestone2/rq1_summary.json)
- The historical water fixture has separate three-scan Remote and Local Hand proofs. Both reach 6.4 L/s from rest with safe modeled samples and credible final observation. A late local operator can miss the deadline even with all downstream components available. The two fixed selector domains have empty intersection. [Hand proof and domains](ATTLEBORO_HAND_THEOREM.md)
- Both controlled water procedures satisfy a common hard contract and have symbolically proved costs for nonnegative weights. The preferred procedure changes with the weights. The selector allows an empty admissible set. [Controlled cost experiment](COMMON_CONTEXT_COST_BENCHMARK.md)
- The HCC experiment proves the bounded research startup and three consecutive credible generation samples under specified forcing. MER6 preserves the controller close command while suppressing contact and electrical generation. [Merwin theorem and source boundary](MERWIN_FORMAL_CASE.md)
- A recorded execution of benign replacement logic establishes bounded device evidence for CHP. Missing physical and customer-service evidence yields UNKNOWN, while a known false process prerequisite rules out its procedure. [CHP results](CHP_FAIL_CLOSED_CASE.md)

## SUPPORTED ONLY WITH QUALIFICATION

- The Merwin result is conditional on a pinned pressure/gate-command trajectory and phase/voltage observations. The speed, torque and generated MW are computed by the reduced fixed-point research plant. The proof uses an ideal grid after contact and sampled safety. An unconditional closed-loop theorem for an installed plant remains open.
- Across water and hydro, the same operational dependency compiler, finite guarded assignments, sequential renderer, executor and prover runner support distinct physical equations and service predicates. Whole-stage semantic equivalence depends on the domain. [Reuse evidence](../results/milestone2/proof_reuse_results.json)
- The scaling timings measure finite linear SMT encodings with one material-balance step. Solver wall time includes process startup for each cell. Declared branch counts describe feasible distinct node valuations. [Scaling experiment](SCALABILITY_RESULTS.md)
- The weight reversal concerns a finite policy choice in a controlled water system. Source thresholds, timings and measurements delimit each historical research fixture.

## NOT YET SUPPORTED

Current-site operating safety, actual staffing or wiring, continuous hydraulics for Attleboro, a complete as-built Merwin electrical and hydraulic model, safe physical CHP restart and restoration of customer supply remain unestablished. The evidence does not quantify real-world attack rates, field error rates, unbounded scalability, global cost optimality, or operational deployment.
